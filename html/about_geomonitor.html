About Geomonitor

Geomonitor is a professional software designed to provide real-time monitoring and information about geological events such as earthquakes and volcanoes. The application aims to offer users an intuitive interface to access data, visualizations, and predictions related to seismic and volcanic activities. Geomonitor provides a comprehensive platform for researchers, emergency responders, and the general public to stay informed about geological events and their potential impacts. It incorporates‰ advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze and interpret geological data to generate probabilistic forecasts with confidence intervals.



Scientific Approach

Geomonitor employs a rigorous scientific approach to monitor and analyze geological events. Our team of experts utilizes cutting-edge technology and methodologies to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the data we provide.

Our machine learning models are trained on 2+ years of seismic data and volcanic eruption historical records to improve prediction accuracy. Models are pre-trained on large datasets to ensure robust performance while advanced users can fine-tune them for specific applications.

Integrated Database


Geomonitor uses a comprehensive internal SQL database that stores and mirrors real-time and historical data from official sources, including the USGS (USA), ESMC (Europe), and INGV (Italy) seismic networks. Historical volcanic eruptions and current alerts are automatically maintained and updated through Mission Control. Full database management utilities are available in the Pro version.

Machine Learning Probability Models


Geomonitor's end goal is prediction. Yes we said that right. Using machine learning models trained on decades of data, GM predicts probabilities for both volcanic eruptions and seismic behavior. Earthquake swarms are classified spatially and compared to historical behavior to generate a probability model for a wide array of outcomes.
