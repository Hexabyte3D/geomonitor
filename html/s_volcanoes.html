Volcanoes


Volcanoes are geological formations that allow molten rock, gases, and ash to escape from beneath the Earth's surface. They are typically found at tectonic plate boundaries or hotspots, where geological activity is high.


Why Should We Monitor Volcanoes?


Monitoring volcanoes is essential for predicting eruptions and ensuring the safety of nearby populations. Geomonitor provides real-time data and visualizations to help users stay informed about volcanic activity around the world.
